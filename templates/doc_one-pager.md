# One-Pager:

## TL;DR

One paragraph summarizing the proposal: what, why, and how.

## Problem

What's broken or missing? Why does it matter now?

## Proposed Solution

What are we doing about it? Keep it concise.

## Key Trade-offs

What are we giving up? What's the cost of inaction?

## Next Steps

What happens after this is approved?
