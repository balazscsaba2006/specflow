# Design Spec:

## Problem

What problem does this design solve?

## Goals

What must this design achieve?

## Non-Goals

What is explicitly out of scope for this design?

## Design

### Overview

High-level approach in 2-3 sentences.

### Key Components

What are the major building blocks?

### Interactions

How do the components interact? What are the key flows?

## Trade-offs

What was traded for what? Why is this the right balance?

## Open Questions

What needs to be resolved before or during implementation?
